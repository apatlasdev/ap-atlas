## BodyParts3D (3D anatomy)
BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International.
- License: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html
- Dataset: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
- Publication: Mitsuhashi N. et al. (2009). BodyParts3D: 3D structure database for anatomical concepts. Nucleic Acids Research. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn613

Adaptations in this project: 401 structures selected and grouped into nursing-level concepts (e.g. the three parts of the deltoid → "Deltoid"), converted from mm/Z-up to metres/Y-up, simplified with quadric decimation to a per-structure triangle budget, positions quantized to 16-bit, packed and gzipped. The spinal cord is a synthetic tube fitted through the vertebral canal (BodyParts3D does not include one). The thyroid gland and peripheral nerves are not in the source set. Adult male reference anatomy; not a complete model of every human structure or variation. Educational, not clinical.

## Eukaryotic Cell Cross Section (3D cell)
"Eukaryotic Cell Cross Section" by dav169, Sketchfab, licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International.
- Model: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/eukaryotic-cell-cross-section-1ed46dbc589d4cb188a016e1456d865e
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adaptations: meshes grouped into 18 organelle parts, simplified from 1.2M to ~376k triangles, textures replaced with flat per-part colors, rotated so the cut face points forward.
